# H2 — Nature as Brand

> Filled 2026-09-19 (session 4). This brief is the contract for the H2 build.

- **Site name / fictional brand:** Understory
- **Niche:** Hotel / forest lodge
- **Target city / region:** Coorg (Kodagu), Karnataka, India — near Madikeri
- **Target customer:** Travellers 25–55 who want to disappear into the forest — couples, remote workers, birders, nature photographers; domestic + international.
- **Business model:** 9-room lodge inside 40 acres of Coorg rainforest; direct bookings; rates include breakfast and one guided walk.
- **Primary CTA:** Reserve a stay (dates, guests, room preference → demo confirmation)
- **Secondary CTA:** Walk the forest (The Forest / Days)
- **Visual thesis:** The forest is the brand. The site reads like a field guide to a living forest: specimen numbers, dotted leaders, small-caps labels, deep greens taken from leaves, not paint. The lodge is organized by forest layers — canopy, understory, forest floor — and so is the page.
- **Reference DNA (max 3):** 1 Hotels (forest-as-identity, restraint), field-guide / herbarium print (specimen numbers, dotted leaders, specimen labels), Juniper Hotel (nature-forward hospitality). Structure/pacing/typography only — no assets, no layouts lifted.
- **Hero concept:** Full-bleed misty rainforest; text top-left — small-caps "Kodagu, Karnataka · 1,200 m", one Fraunces line "The forest is the brand.", short intro, two text CTAs. Bottom-right: a specimen label ("Understory — the layer we live in"). The image drifts slowly (translateX 2% → 0 over ~2.4s), like wind through the canopy.
- **Page list:** Home · The Forest · Stays (list) · Stay detail ×5 (Fern, Moss, Fig, Bamboo, Canopy) · Days · The Table · Field Notes (list) · Field Notes detail ×4 · The Understory (gallery) · Contact/Reserve.
- **Signature interaction:** "The drift" — hero image pans slowly like wind; dotted leaders draw in as sections enter; everything else is a slow rise (Reveal). No parallax, no scroll choreography.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display, 300–600, optical sizing) + Archivo (UI/body, 400–600). Labels: 11px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase.
- **Color system:** paper `#F1F2EC` · ink `#1C241D` · mist `#E4E7DB` · stone `#C9CFBC` · fern `#5A7050` (large/decorative only) · fern-deep `#3D5233` (text-safe accent) · bark `#2A3527` (dark sections) · sage `#A9B79A` (text on bark). No other hues.
- **Photography direction:** mist, canopy light, ferns, moss, bamboo, waterfalls, shade-grown coffee, timber interiors, dawn. No stock "happy" faces, no bright tropical color, no resort pools.
- **Trust/proof elements:** none fabricated. Neutral facts only (9 rooms, 40 acres, one table, 1,200 m). Demo contact details marked as demo. No awards, no ratings, no testimonials.
- **Mobile behavior:** single column, full-bleed images, sticky reserve bar at bottom, full-screen menu, no horizontal overflow at 360px.
- **Accessibility considerations:** skip link, visible focus, alt text, labeled forms, reduced-motion disables drift/reveals, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text.
- **SEO/local SEO needs:** per-page title/meta, canonical, LodgingBusiness JSON-LD (address, geo, hours, priceRange), city/service language, hours + location + how-to-reach (Madikeri, Coimbatore/Bengaluru airports, rail).
- **Asset list:** `docs/assets/H2.md` — verified URLs only (CC/PDM, attribution per license).
- **Do not use:** solid hairline rules as the primary motif (that is H1's identity), cream/bronze palette, Cormorant, scale-settle motion, promo badges, parallax, glassmorphism, decorative gradients, shadowed cards, more than 2 fonts, auto-playing anything, fake reviews/awards, lorem ipsum.
- **Acceptance checklist:** 12-dimension QA gate (Bible §14) + H2 Avoid list + 30-Site Test overlap check against H1.

## Creative direction (one paragraph)

Understory is a lodge that does not compete with the forest — it files itself under it. The site
reads like a field guide: numbered specimens, dotted leaders, small-caps labels, and deep greens
taken from the leaves rather than the paint. The hero is mist in the canopy and a single line that
is the thesis — the forest is the brand. Every section after that is a layer: canopy, understory,
forest floor, each a full-bleed plate with a number and a quiet note. The rooms are named for the
plants they open onto. Motion is weather, not machinery: the image drifts, the dotted rules draw
in, the text rises. Nothing announces itself. The forest does the talking.
