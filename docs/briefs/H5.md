# H5 — Experiential Escape

> Filled 2026-09-19 (session 6). This brief is the contract for the H5 build.

- **Site name / fictional brand:** Safar
- **Niche:** Hotel / experiential escape (a high-altitude house where the stay is a passage, not a destination)
- **Target city / region:** Leh, Ladakh, India — the high-altitude road country (the Indus valley, the passes, the cold lakes)
- **Target customer:** Travellers 28–60 who want the journey itself — the road, the pass, the cold light — not just a bed; couples, solo travellers, long-stay writers, photographers; domestic + international.
- **Business model:** A five-stay high-altitude house on the Leh road, kept by a small family since 1998; direct bookings; rates include breakfast and one guided passage (a walk of the road, the pass, or the lake).
- **Primary CTA:** Reserve a stay (dates, guests, stay preference → demo confirmation)
- **Secondary CTA:** Walk the road (The Road / The Passage)
- **Visual thesis:** The road is the brand. The site reads like a route log and an altitude chart: a wide, expansive display face, small-caps route labels, numbered waypoints (Stage 01, Stage 02…), and elevation markers (m). The palette is taken from Ladakh itself — glacial ice, high-altitude blue, and a single warm earth accent used only where the sun would catch the cold. The experience is in the restraint: no badges, no cards, no noise.
- **Reference DNA (max 3):** The Manali–Leh highway (the road as the point), Pangong Tso (the cold lake, the altitude), the Ladakh monasteries (the passage, the climb). Structure/pacing/typography only — no assets, no layouts lifted.
- **Hero concept:** Full-bleed Ladakh road at first light (cold, high-altitude, the road stretching out); text top-left — small-caps route label "SAFAR · LEH 3,500 M", one Unbounded line "The road is the point.", short intro, two text CTAs. Bottom-right: a small route line with the family line ("Safar — est. 1998"). The image ascends slowly (a quiet rise, ~2.4s), like climbing out of the valley.
- **Page list:** Home · The Passage (story/why) · Stays (list) · Stay detail ×5 (The Approach, The Pass, The Valley, The Lake, The Return) · The Table (the high-altitude kitchen) · The Road (the experiences — passes, lakes, monasteries, stars) · The Altitude (the gallery — light, cold, sky) · A Route (the rhythm of a stay — a day at the house) · Contact/Reserve.
- **Signature interaction:** "The ascent" — a slow, deliberate rise (translateY 24px → 0 + opacity) as sections enter the viewport, like climbing out of a valley; a route line draws in (like a road being traced) across section dividers; everything else is a quiet rise (Reveal). No parallax, no scroll choreography.
- **Typography:** Unbounded (display, 400–700, wide expansive face) + Manrope (UI/body, 400–700). Labels: 11px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase.
- **Color system:** ice `#EEF1F4` (bg) · slate `#232B36` (ink) · mist `#DCE3EA` (surface) · stone `#B8C2CC` (border) · sky `#2E5A7A` (high-altitude blue, large/decorative only) · sky-deep `#24485F` (text-safe accent) · earth `#B5623A` (Ladakh earth, large/decorative only) · error `#A8442E`. No other hues.
- **Photography direction:** the road, the passes, the cold lakes, the monasteries, the high-altitude sky, the cold light, the earth. No stock "happy" faces, no bright tropical color, no resort pools.
- **Trust/proof elements:** none fabricated. Neutral facts only (5 stays, 1 road, 1 pass, est. 1998). Demo contact details marked as demo. No awards, no ratings, no testimonials.
- **Mobile behavior:** single column, full-bleed images, sticky reserve bar at bottom, full-screen menu, no horizontal overflow at 360px.
- **Accessibility considerations:** skip link, visible focus, alt text, labeled forms, reduced-motion disables ascent/reveals, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text.
- **SEO/local SEO needs:** per-page title/meta, canonical, LodgingBusiness JSON-LD (address, geo, hours, priceRange), city/service language, hours + location + how-to-reach (Leh, Leh airport, Manali/Srinagar roads).
- **Asset list:** `docs/assets/H5.md` — verified URLs only (CC/PDM, attribution per license).
- **Do not use:** solid hairline rules as the primary motif (that is H1's identity), dotted leaders + specimen numbers (that is H2's identity), diamond ornament + numbered lineage (that is H3's identity), sprocket + timecode (that is H4's identity), cream/bronze palette (H1), forest-green palette (H2), ivory/maroon/gold palette (H3), charcoal/silver/tungsten palette (H4), Cormorant (H1), Fraunces (H2), Marcellus (H3), Bebas Neue (H4), "settle" motion (H1), "drift" motion (H2), "gild" motion (H3), "flicker"/"fade" motion (H4), promo badges, parallax, glassmorphism, decorative gradients, shadowed cards, more than 2 fonts, auto-playing anything, fake reviews/awards, lorem ipsum.
- **Acceptance checklist:** 12-dimension QA gate (Bible §14) + H5 Avoid list + 30-Site Test overlap check against H1, H2, H3, and H4.

## Creative direction (one paragraph)

Safar is a house that is a passage, not a destination. The site should feel like opening a route
log: one full-bleed image of the cold road at first light, a single expansive line — the road is
the point — a small route line, and a quiet invitation to reserve. Every section after that is an
act of the journey — the passage, the stays, the table, the road, the altitude, the route — each a
full-bleed plate with a waypoint number and a quiet note. There are no cards, no badges, no
gradients, no motion that announces itself. The page moves at the pace of a climb: a slow ascent,
a quiet rise, a pause. The road does the talking; the family has been keeping it for a quarter of
a century, and it will keep it after the guest is gone.
