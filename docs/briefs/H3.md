# H3 — Heritage Grandeur

> Filled 2026-09-19 (session 5). This brief is the contract for the H3 build.

- **Site name / fictional brand:** The Alkari
- **Niche:** Hotel / heritage haveli (palace)
- **Target city / region:** Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, India — inside the fort (the Golden City)
- **Target customer:** Travellers 30–65 who want to stay inside a living heritage — couples, anniversary guests, heritage collectors, long-stay writers; domestic + international.
- **Business model:** 5-suite haveli inside the Jaisalmer fort, kept by the Alkari family for six generations; direct bookings; rates include breakfast and one heritage hour (a guided walk of the haveli and the fort).
- **Primary CTA:** Reserve a stay (dates, guests, suite preference → demo confirmation)
- **Secondary CTA:** Walk the haveli (The Haveli / The Courtyards)
- **Visual thesis:** The haveli is the brand. The site reads like a family ledger and a court record: a stately serif, small-caps labels, a numbered lineage, and a single refined ornament (a diamond) between sections. The palette is taken from Jaisalmer itself — ivory, sandstone, and a deep maroon that is the family's colour — with gold used only where light would catch it. Grandeur is in the restraint: no badges, no cards, no noise.
- **Reference DNA (max 3):** The Samode Haveli (Jaipur — ornate-but-refined haveli), the Jaisalmer Fort Palace (sandstone grandeur, the fort), the Wadiyar court of Mysore (lineage, ceremony, the ledger). Structure/pacing/typography only — no assets, no layouts lifted.
- **Hero concept:** Full-bleed golden haveli facade at first light; text top-left — small-caps "Jaisalmer · the fort", one Marcellus line "Kept for six generations.", short intro, two text CTAs. Bottom-right: a small diamond ornament with the family line ("The Alkari — est. 1874"). The image gilds slowly (a soft light sweep across the gold, ~2.4s), like the sun finding the sandstone.
- **Page list:** Home · The Haveli (lineage/history) · Suites (list) · Suite detail ×5 (Durbar, Jharokha, Peacock, Courtyard, Sandstone) · The Courtyards · The Banquet · The Ledger (list) · Ledger detail ×4 · The Collection (gallery) · Contact/Reserve.
- **Signature interaction:** "The gild" — a slow, low-amplitude light sweep across the gold ornaments and hero imagery as they enter the viewport; the diamond ornament draws in; everything else is a stately rise (Reveal). No parallax, no scroll choreography.
- **Typography:** Marcellus (display, 400, stately Roman serif) + Karla (UI/body, 400–700). Labels: 11px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase.
- **Color system:** ivory `#F3ECDD` · ink `#221812` · mist `#E8DECB` · sandstone `#C7B28C` · maroon `#6E2A2A` (large/decorative only) · maroon-deep `#521F1F` (text-safe accent) · gold `#B0854A` (large/decorative only) · espresso `#33211C` (dark sections) · error `#8A3B2E`. No other hues.
- **Photography direction:** sandstone havelis, courtyards, jharokhas, peacocks, the fort, the desert, handwork, gold, dawn. No stock "happy" faces, no bright tropical color, no resort pools.
- **Trust/proof elements:** none fabricated. Neutral facts only (5 suites, 6 generations, 1 courtyard, est. 1874). Demo contact details marked as demo. No awards, no ratings, no testimonials.
- **Mobile behavior:** single column, full-bleed images, sticky reserve bar at bottom, full-screen menu, no horizontal overflow at 360px.
- **Accessibility considerations:** skip link, visible focus, alt text, labeled forms, reduced-motion disables gild/reveals, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text.
- **SEO/local SEO needs:** per-page title/meta, canonical, LodgingBusiness JSON-LD (address, geo, hours, priceRange), city/service language, hours + location + how-to-reach (Jaisalmer, Jodhpur/Jaipur airports, rail).
- **Asset list:** `docs/assets/H3.md` — verified URLs only (CC/PDM, attribution per license).
- **Do not use:** solid hairline rules as the primary motif (that is H1's identity), dotted leaders + specimen numbers (that is H2's identity), cream/bronze palette (H1), forest-green palette (H2), Cormorant (H1), Fraunces (H2), "settle" motion (H1), "drift" motion (H2), promo badges, parallax, glassmorphism, decorative gradients, shadowed cards, more than 2 fonts, auto-playing anything, fake reviews/awards, lorem ipsum.
- **Acceptance checklist:** 12-dimension QA gate (Bible §14) + H3 Avoid list + 30-Site Test overlap check against H1 and H2.

## Creative direction (one paragraph)

The Alkari is a haveli that has been kept, not built. The site should feel like opening a
family ledger: one full-bleed image of the golden facade at first light, a single stately line —
kept for six generations — a small diamond, and a quiet invitation to reserve. Every section after
that is an act of record — the lineage, the suites, the courtyards, the banquet, the ledger, the
collection — each a full-bleed plate with a number and a quiet note. There are no cards, no badges,
no gradients, no motion that announces itself. The page moves at the pace of a court: a slow gild,
a stately rise, a pause. The haveli does the talking; the family has been keeping it for a
hundred and fifty years, and it will keep it after the guest is gone.
