# H4 — Cinematic Boutique

> Filled 2026-09-19 (session 6). This brief is the contract for the H4 build.

- **Site name / fictional brand:** Nocturne
- **Niche:** Hotel / boutique
- **Target city / region:** Mumbai, Maharashtra, India — Fort / Ballard Estate, the old film district (the city of cinema)
- **Target customer:** Travellers 25–55 who want a stay that feels like a film — couples, creatives, film people, night owls; domestic + international.
- **Business model:** 12-room boutique hotel in the old film district of Mumbai; direct bookings; rates include breakfast and one screening (a private showing in the house's cinema).
- **Primary CTA:** Reserve a stay (dates, guests, room preference → demo confirmation)
- **Secondary CTA:** Watch the reel (The House / The Projection / The Archive)
- **Visual thesis:** The night is the brand. The site reads like a film title card and a projection room: a condensed poster face, timecode labels, a sprocket-hole leader, and a single warm tungsten light against charcoal. The palette is taken from a projection room — charcoal, silver, and the amber of the projector — with the dark doing the work. Cinematic is in the restraint: no badges, no cards, no noise, and the page is dark-first, the one site in the set that lives at night.
- **Reference DNA (max 3):** a 1960s Bombay talkie title card (condensed caps, a single warm light), the projection room of an old single-screen cinema (charcoal, the amber lamp, the reel), the night city (Mumbai after dark, wet streets, neon). Structure/pacing/typography only — no assets, no layouts lifted.
- **Hero concept:** Full-bleed Mumbai at night, wet street and distant lights; text top-left — a timecode "TC 00:00:00:00", one Bebas line "The night is the brand.", short intro, two text CTAs. Bottom-right: a small reel mark with the house line ("Nocturne — est. 1962"). The image flickers faintly (a low-amplitude projector flicker, ~1.6s), like a lamp finding the screen.
- **Page list:** Home · The House (story) · Rooms (list) · Room detail ×5 (The Lobby, The Reel, The Cut, The Fade, The Frame) · The Bar · The Projection (private cinema) · The Archive (gallery of frames) · A Day (the rhythm of a stay) · Contact/Reserve.
- **Signature interaction:** "The flicker" — a faint, low-amplitude opacity flicker on the hero image as it enters, like a projector lamp; "the fade" — sections fade up from black (opacity 0 → 1, no translate) as they enter. Everything else is a quiet rise (Reveal). No parallax, no scroll choreography.
- **Typography:** Bebas Neue (display, 400, condensed poster caps) + Space Grotesk (UI/body, 400–600). Labels: 11px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase. Timecodes: Space Grotesk, tabular-nums.
- **Color system:** charcoal `#141519` (background) · smoke `#1F2127` (surface) · graphite `#2A2D35` (borders) · silver `#C6CAD2` (body text on dark) · screen `#F1EFEA` (headings / bright text) · tungsten `#D99A4E` (large/decorative + accent) · tungsten-deep `#9C7138` (text-safe accent on light surfaces) · error `#C65B45`. No other hues.
- **Photography direction:** Mumbai at night, wet streets, neon, the old film district, a projection room, a bar at night, a dark hotel room with a single warm light, the sea at night, monochrome city. No stock "happy" faces, no bright daylight resort color, no pools.
- **Trust/proof elements:** none fabricated. Neutral facts only (12 rooms, 1 cinema, 1 bar, est. 1962). Demo contact details marked as demo. No awards, no ratings, no testimonials.
- **Mobile behavior:** single column, full-bleed images, sticky reserve bar at bottom, full-screen menu, no horizontal overflow at 360px.
- **Accessibility considerations:** skip link, visible focus, alt text, labeled forms, reduced-motion disables flicker/fade, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text (silver on charcoal).
- **SEO/local SEO needs:** per-page title/meta, canonical, LodgingBusiness JSON-LD (address, geo, hours, priceRange), city/service language, hours + location + how-to-reach (Mumbai, Mumbai airport, rail).
- **Asset list:** `docs/assets/H4.md` — verified URLs only (CC/PDM, attribution per license).

## Creative direction (one paragraph)

Nocturne is a house that only really wakes up after dark. The site should feel like the
moment the lights go down in a single-screen cinema: one full-bleed image of the city at
night, a single condensed line — the night is the brand — a sprocket-hole leader, and a
quiet invitation to reserve. Every section after that is a scene: the house, the rooms, the
bar, the projection, the archive, the day — each a full-bleed plate with a timecode and a
quiet note. There are no cards, no badges, no gradients, no motion that announces itself.
The page moves at the pace of a film: a faint flicker, a slow fade, a cut. The night does
the talking; the house has been keeping it since 1962, and it will keep it after the guest
is gone.
