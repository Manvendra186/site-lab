# H1 — Quiet Luxury Retreat

> Filled 2026-09-18 (session 1). This brief is the contract for the H1 build.

- **Site name / fictional brand:** Sthira
- **Niche:** Hotel / boutique lakeside retreat
- **Target city / region:** Udaipur, Rajasthan, India
- **Target customer:** Travellers 30–60 seeking stillness over spectacle — couples, long-stay writers, quiet-luxury buyers; domestic + international.
- **Business model:** 14-room private lakeside house; direct bookings; rates include breakfast and one experience per stay.
- **Primary CTA:** Reserve a stay (dates, guests, room preference → demo confirmation)
- **Secondary CTA:** Explore the house (About / Rooms / Experiences)
- **Visual thesis:** Stillness as luxury. The house is quiet, so the page must be quiet: full-bleed photography, hairline rules, generous whitespace, almost no cards, zero badges. The only motion is a slow settle.
- **Reference DNA (max 3):** Aman (stillness, cream/charcoal, serif, thin rules, almost no cards), The Peninsula (heritage calm), 1 Hotels (restraint). Structure/pacing/typography only — no assets, no layouts lifted.
- **Hero concept:** Full-bleed lake at first light; small-caps location label; one serif line — "Stillness, kept."; hairline; two text CTAs. Image settles from 1.04 → 1.00 over ~1.6s.
- **Page list:** Home · About (The House) · Rooms (list) · Room detail ×6 · Experiences · Dining · Wellness · Gallery · Journal (list) · Journal detail ×4 · Contact/Reserve.
- **Signature interaction:** "The settle" — hero image scale-settle + hairline rules that draw in as sections enter; everything else is a slow fade. No parallax, no scroll choreography.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (display, 300–500) + Instrument Sans (UI/body, 400–500). Labels: 11px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase.
- **Color system:** paper `#F4F0E8` · ink `#1C1B18` · mist `#E9E4D8` · stone `#D8D2C4` · bronze `#8A6F4D` (large/decorative only) · bronze-deep `#6B5233` (text-safe accent). No other hues.
- **Photography direction:** dawn light, water, linen, stone, wood, quiet interiors. No pools-with-people, no stock "happy" faces, no bright tropical color.
- **Trust/proof elements:** none fabricated. Neutral facts only (14 rooms, 2 acres, one seating). Demo contact details marked as demo. No awards, no ratings, no testimonials.
- **Mobile behavior:** single column, full-bleed images, sticky hairline reserve bar at bottom, full-screen serif menu, no horizontal overflow at 360px.
- **Accessibility considerations:** skip link, visible focus, alt text, labeled forms, reduced-motion disables settle/reveals, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text.
- **SEO/local SEO needs:** per-page title/meta, canonical, LodgingBusiness JSON-LD (address, geo, hours, priceRange), city/service language, hours + location + how-to-reach (transport/parking).
- **Asset list:** `docs/assets/H1.md` — verified Unsplash URLs only (Unsplash License, attribution).
- **Do not use:** promo badges, parallax, glassmorphism, gradients, shadowed cards, more than 2 fonts, auto-playing anything, fake reviews/awards, lorem ipsum.
- **Acceptance checklist:** 12-dimension QA gate (Bible §14) + H1 Avoid list + 30-Site Test overlap check.

## Creative direction (one paragraph)

Sthira is a house that asks nothing of you. The site should feel like stepping off a boat:
one full-bleed image of water at first light, a single serif sentence, a hairline, and a quiet
invitation to reserve. Every section after that is an act of subtraction — photography carries
the story, typography carries the meaning, and a 1px rule is the only ornament. There are no
cards, no badges, no gradients, no motion that announces itself. The page breathes at the pace
of a lake: slow fades, a settle, a pause.

## Text mood board (5–8 tokens)

`serif stillness` · `slow settle` · `no badges, no cards`
