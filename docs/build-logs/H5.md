## Implementation notes (built 2026-09-19)

1. **`await params` from the start** — the single dynamic route (`stays/[slug]/page.tsx`) types
   `params: Promise<{ slug: string }>` and `await params` in both `generateMetadata` and the page
   component (decision #18). This avoids the H2 dev-only bug where a synchronous `params.slug` read
   404'd every dynamic route under Playwright while the SSG build still passed.
2. **Identity kept distinct** — light-first ice/slate/sky palette with a single earth accent,
   Unbounded + Manrope, the "ascent" (slow rise, translateY 24px→0 + opacity) motion, and the
   waypoint / stage-label motif (Stage 01–05). Deliberately avoids H1's hairline rules, H2's
   dotted-leader/specimen motif, H3's diamond ornament + numbered lineage, and H4's sprocket-hole
   leader + timecode (brief §Do-not-use).
3. **Shared primitives only** — `Container`, `Img`, `Reveal`/`useInView`/`usePrefersReducedMotion`,
   the form kit (RHF + zod), and the SEO/JSON-LD helpers from `@site-lab/shared`; no visual language
   leaks in from the shared package.
4. **Light-first, the only high-altitude day site in the set** — `color-scheme: light`, ice
   background, slate body text, a single high-altitude blue accent (sky) with an earth secondary.
   The hero carries the ascent; the text sits above the image so it is always readable.
5. **Demo-only reservation** — the reserve form validates client-side (RHF + zod) and reaches a
   success state; nothing is sent or stored (Bible §10). No fake reviews, awards, badges, gradients,
   or glassmorphism.

**Minor, acceptable echoes:**
- **H1 (cream) and H5 (ice) are both light-first neutrals.** H1 is warm cream with a bronze accent
  (Udaipur, heritage); H5 is cool ice with a high-altitude blue accent (Ladakh, high country). The
  ground temperature and accent hue are the differentiators — the two sites read as warm-heritage
  vs cool-high-altitude, not "same template, different colors."
- **H5's stage labels (Stage 01–05)** are a small numbered waypoint marker, not a signature motif.
  H2's specimen numbers and H3's numbered lineage are full identity systems; H5's is a quiet journey
  index. H5's signature motif is the waypoint / stage-label system + the ascent motion.
- **H2/H3/H4/H5 all target `/reserve`** as the primary CTA. This is a conventional path, not a
  lifted asset or layout.

**Verdict:** H5 is clearly distinct from H1, H2, H3 and H4 on palette (cool light-first
high-altitude blue), type, motion, motif, city, stay concept, and voice. No re-art-direction
required.
